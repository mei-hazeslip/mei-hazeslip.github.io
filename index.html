About Me

I am a data scientist and PhD at Rice University interested in machine learning, time-series analysis, and data visualization.

My research uses seismic signals to image Earth's structure and understand earthquake processes, while my data science projects focus on predictive modeling, forecasting, and practical decision support.

I enjoy turning noisy, complex datasets into clear stories, reproducible workflows, and visual explanations that help people make better decisions.
